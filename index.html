FAQ

Questions people ask

What is $NEAGRA?▾

A meme coin from the Afro in Transit Foundation, built for entertainment and community pride. It is not an investment product, and holding it is not equity in the Foundation.

Can I use this to send money to family?▾

No. $NEAGRA is not a remittance or money-transfer service and isn't licensed to move money on anyone's behalf. Solana's low network fees are a property of the blockchain, not a financial service this project operates.

What's the Community Wallet?▾

10% of supply, held in a public wallet the team may draw from at its own discretion to support community initiatives it chooses to highlight. It isn't managed by a registered charity, has no guaranteed distributions, and holders have no legal claim over it.

Are influencers paid to promote this?▾

Some marketing partners may be compensated. They're required to disclose that wherever they post about $NEAGRA.

Is my money safe?▾

Meme coins carry high risk and can lose value fast. Only ever risk what you can afford to lose, and never share your seed phrase with anyone.
